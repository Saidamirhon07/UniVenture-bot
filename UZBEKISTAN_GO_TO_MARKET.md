# UniVentureAI — Uzbekistan paid launch plan

## Offer and price

Launch with one plan so the purchase decision stays simple:

**Founding Pro: 799 Telegram Stars every 30 days**

After the first 100 paying members or 30 days, test **999 Stars** against 799. Do not change the price based on opinions; compare completed checkout rate, acquisition cost, weekly activity and refund rate for each cohort.

The launch position is: **“Your complete university application system in Telegram.”** Sell the outcome of organized weekly progress, not a list of AI features.

Why the entry price is credible:

- Instagram's potential advertising reach in Uzbekistan was 11.7 million in early 2025, giving the product a large local discovery channel.
- Current online IELTS course listings commonly show total prices above 1.5 million UZS, while the UniVentureAI founders price is a smaller monthly decision and covers admissions, SAT and IELTS in one product.
- At the September 8, 2026 official exchange-rate report of 11,789.33 UZS per USD, an approximate $16 Stars purchase is around 189,000 UZS. The exact buyer cost varies by platform and where Stars are purchased.

Sources: [Telegram digital-goods payments](https://core.telegram.org/bots/payments-stars), [Digital 2025 Uzbekistan](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-uzbekistan), [IELTS course listings](https://kurshub.uz/kursy/kursy-po-anglijskomu-ielts/), [September 8 exchange-rate report](https://zamin.uz/en/economy/220510-exchange-rates-for-september-8-announced.html).

## Revenue target math

At an estimated buyer spend near $16 for 799 Stars:

| First-month result | Paying users needed | Approximate customer spend |
|---|---:|---:|
| $2,000 gross sales | 125 | about 23.6m UZS |
| $2,500 gross sales | 157 | about 29.5m UZS |
| $3,000 gross sales | 188 | about 35.4m UZS |

Telegram Stars cash-out value can be lower than what a buyer spends because the user's purchase channel, app-store fees, holding period and conversion route affect proceeds. If the goal means cash proceeds after the Stars system, plan for roughly **190–285 customers**, then verify the actual payout rate in your Telegram/Fragment account before setting a hard budget.

This is an aggressive target for a first month. It needs founder-led sales, partners and daily proof content; ads alone are unlikely to deliver it reliably.

## The first 48 hours

The goal is 10–20 real buyers and five usable testimonials.

1. Build a warm list of 150 people before launch: past bot users, school friends, applicants, parents, teachers and counselors. Tag each as student, parent or partner.
2. Personally message the 40 strongest fits with a 20-second screen recording and one specific reason the product fits their current problem.
3. Run a 30-minute live Telegram/Instagram demo: create a profile, evaluate one anonymized essay, open a personalized plan, complete one SAT or IELTS task, and pay inside Telegram.
4. Offer the first 100 members the locked 799-Star founders price plus one weekly group application clinic for the first month.
5. Ask each first buyer one question after the first successful task: “What became easier today?” Turn the answer into a short approved testimonial.

Do not lead with “AI admissions platform.” Lead with a visible problem:

- “You have 12 application tasks and no idea what to do today.”
- “Your essay sounds correct but forgettable.”
- “You practise SAT/IELTS but repeat the same mistakes.”
- “Your parents ask for progress and you cannot show it.”

## Four acquisition engines

### 1. Instagram proof content

Publish two Reels and five Stories daily for the first 14 days. Each Reel should show the product within the first two seconds.

Content rotation:

- **Before/after:** one weak EC description transformed into evidence and impact.
- **Live score repair:** one SAT or IELTS mistake, explanation, retry and streak.
- **Application teardown:** three reasons a sample profile feels scattered, then the Roadmap fixes the order.
- **Tool reveal:** one 12–18 second recording of Essay Review, School Fit, Venture or Flight Plan.
- **Founder build log:** one real bug fixed or student suggestion shipped.

Every post uses one call to action: **“Send ROADMAP in DM.”** Reply with a short qualifying question, a demo clip and the Telegram bot link.

### 2. Telegram community distribution

Buy or negotiate posts only in channels whose recent posts show real comments/views from IELTS, SAT, scholarships, study-abroad or academic-lyceum audiences. Use a unique start link for each placement and stop channels that do not create checkout starts within 48 hours.

Use links such as `https://t.me/YOUR_BOT?start=ig_reel01`, `?start=tg_channelname`, and `?start=partner_school1`. Check `/sales` daily to compare confirmed Stars payments by first-touch source.

The placement creative should be a native mini-case, not a banner: problem, 12-second product proof, founders price, direct bot link.

### 3. Counselors, teachers and learning centers

Recruit 20 partners in month one. Give them a live demo, a partner code, approved visuals and a 15% reward for each first payment. Position UniVentureAI as the daily workspace between lessons: teachers keep teaching; the app handles practice, reminders, drafts and progress.

Priority partners: IELTS/SAT teachers, study-abroad counselors, private schools, academic lyceums, debate/MUN communities and university student mentors.

### 4. Student ambassador loop

Select 10 active students from different schools. Each runs one small demo for classmates and produces one honest screen-recorded use case per week. Reward only verified paid referrals. A later product release should automate referral attribution and bonus access; track the launch cohort manually in a sheet until that system exists.

## Working launch budget

Start with **8–10 million UZS**, released in small batches:

| Use | Budget |
|---|---:|
| Micro-creators and student pages | 2.5m UZS |
| Instagram retargeting and conversion ads | 2.0m UZS |
| Telegram channel placements | 1.5m UZS |
| School demos and small events | 1.0m UZS |
| Creative production and contingency | 1.0–3.0m UZS |

Keep success-based partner rewards outside this fixed budget. Pause any paid source when its cost per completed subscription passes **70,000 UZS** until the creative or audience is changed.

The strongest investor story after month one is not “we made an AI app.” It is: qualified acquisition cost, paid conversion, weekly retention, completed student work, renewal rate and approved outcome stories. These numbers will show whether the product can scale.
